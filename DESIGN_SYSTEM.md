# Trip Packing Planner Design System

This prototype now follows a travel editorial mobile system inspired by the provided references.

## Direction
- Airy, premium travel utility: lots of breathing room, quiet metadata, photographic / glassy surfaces, soft shadows.
- Mobile cards feel like floating travel panels, not heavy dashboard cards.
- Keep controls useful and testable, but make them softer: pill inputs, compact icon buttons, selected states with textured light blue.
- The suitcase is the visual centerpiece. Prefer dimensional, tactile visualization over flat wireframe diagrams.

## Palette
- Paper: `#f7f8f5`
- Mist: `#dfecee`
- Card: translucent white
- Ink: `#111111`
- Muted text: `#7b807d`
- Line: `rgba(18, 26, 25, 0.1)`
- Highlight: textured light blue `#dceff7`
- Warning: soft rose `#f3ded9`

## Shape And Type
- Large confident mobile headings, tighter editorial labels.
- Cards use 26-34px radius.
- Inputs and primary controls use full pill radii.
- Small secondary metadata stays light and precise.

## Interaction
- Primary actions are black or dark glass.
- Selected / recommended states use light blue selection surfaces.
- 3D suitcase visualization should stay interactive and explainable, with tappable item chips and item detail cards.
